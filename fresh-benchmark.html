02 WHERE WE FALL SHORT

Names need better ordering and coverage.

31 / 40 names

The spelling often exists, but ranks too low

Only 9 names match at rank one, although 31 match within five. That leaves 22 name inputs with the intended spelling available below the first suggestion. Examples: nivika → निविका (rank 2), aarvika → आर्विका (rank 2), urvika → उर्विका (rank 3).

9 / 40 names

Some spellings are absent from five suggestions

All nine of our top-five misses are names. Google finds seven of those. Conjuncts and nasal/vowel choices recur in examples such as aadvika → आद्विका, saanvika → सान्विका and aayanshika → आयांशिका. These are observed patterns, not proven internal causes.

14 Google-only first matches

The name gap is visible in paired cases

On first suggestions, Google alone matches 14 names, we alone match 4, both match 5, and neither matches 17. Across all inputs, 26 of our 35 first-suggestion misses are present at ranks 2–5; nine remain absent.

No fixes in this evaluation

The engine, rules, seed lexicon and ranking configuration are unchanged. This set is now observed and must become a regression set if used for future improvements. A new unseen test is needed to measure those improvements.

05 REPRODUCE AND AUDIT

References first. Predictions second.

A frozen population

315 authored candidate cases; 184 rejected for earlier exposure. Seeded hash ordering, fixed category quotas, and one input per lexical family yielded 89 cases. Category quotas were not filled by relaxing freshness. Two uncertain household families were corrected or dropped during pre-evaluation review, before predictions.

Full training membership screening

All 2,397,414 raw TRAIN rows were screened, including rows excluded by some model builders. Roman spelling uses the engine’s conservative normalization; native spelling uses NFC and removes joiners. Every accepted reference must be absent.

Shared scoring rules

Same Roman token, first five unique candidates, NFC exact match, all predeclared references accepted. Errors remain misses. Character error is micro Unicode code-point edit distance. Timing is not ranked: our Node computation and Google network requests measure different things.

Reproducible evidence

Model and source hashes are checked before and after evaluation. Google raw responses, case labels, rejected candidates and selection evidence are retained. Primary scores were independently reconciled in Python. Intervals describe this sample under independence assumptions, not population representativeness.
